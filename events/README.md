# Tent & Table

A phone-friendly web app for running a ministry's events, from the annual tent revival to a monthly board meeting.

## How It's Organized

- **One login, one ministry.** A ministry account holds up to 10 logins. Each login (email) belongs to exactly one ministry; someone who serves with two ministries signs up for each with a different email. The database enforces this (`ed_members.user_id` is unique).
- **Directory** (the ministry's pool), in tabs: People (team members with titles, worship, speakers, volunteers; status Current or Previous), Vendors, Contacts, Equipment inventory, and Budget by year. Every person, vendor and contact can be called, texted, emailed, mapped or visited from their card.
- **New events** start with a short walkthrough: name, dates and times, place and address, a yes or no for each part the event will have (guests, volunteers, speakers, worship, team, meals, vendors, equipment, money, connection cards, schedule, agenda; tasks are on for every event), goals (volunteers needed, seats, budget) and the text for the share pages. Saving builds the event, a general volunteer spot sized to the goal, and an RSVP for each day, so the QR codes work right away. The volunteer goal is tracked on the event and shown on the volunteer page.
- **Contacts.** The Directory's Contacts tab holds every person and business in one list. Each contact has a type, the same five used inside every event: Team (leaders, board, staff, worship, speakers, prayer team), Volunteer, Guest, Vendor and Other (landowners, permit offices, partners). Filter by type or by a team group like Board, and every row reads the same way: the name, then what they do and how to reach them, with the type on the right. Volunteers and guests who sign up can be saved to Contacts in one tap.
- **Events.** Each event opens in its own workspace organized into areas: Overview (countdown, the heart of the event, at-a-glance numbers, what needs attention, what is coming up), Plan (tasks, schedule, agenda, set lists, equipment), Contacts (Team, Volunteers with meal sign-ups, Guests with RSVPs, check-in and follow-up, Vendors, and Other), Prayer and Money. Each list carries its own share link and QR code: volunteers and meals, guest RSVP, and the connection card. An event pulls what it needs from the Directory: the people serving and their role, vendors with cost, payment status, who pays and when it's due, contacts, and how many of each equipment item. Equipment that is double-booked across overlapping events is flagged.
- **Tasks.** Every event has a task list (Plan > Tasks) for everything from planning and prep to setup, the event itself, teardown and after. Each task has two categories: a segment (Planning, Prep, Setup, During The Event, Teardown, After The Event) and an area it affects (Hospitality, Food, Stage, AV And Sound, Worship, Kids, Parking, Security, Prayer, Decor, Setup Crew, Admin, or your own). Each task records who created it, can be assigned to several contacts (team members, board, prayer team, volunteers, vendors or other contacts; not guests, worship or speakers), or to typed names, and says how many people it needs, so a four-person job shows how many more hands it still needs. View the list by segment or by area, filter to one area, and print or copy it from the Share page, all of it or just one team's list.
- **The heart of the event.** Each event can have a theme and a purpose ("The purpose of this event is to give God glory…"). They show at the top of the Overview with the event's key verse, for the team only, never on public pages.
- **Prayer.** Turn on Prayer for an event to get a Prayer area with three lists: Intercession (the people and needs the prayer team carries), Declarations and Scripture. Nothing here is checked off; an item stays until you delete it. A need can name anyone in Contacts, have a follow-up date (it shows under Needs Attention when due), be kept private, and record What God Did, which moves it to its own list. A contact's card shows every need linked to them. The Prayer Guide puts the theme, scripture, declarations, needs and answered prayer on one page to copy, share or print, for the prayer team or as a recap after the event. Private needs are left off. Duplicating an event carries its scripture and declarations, not its needs.
- **Agenda and schedule leaders.** Each agenda segment (Opening Prayer, Worship, Housekeeping, The Message, anything you name it) has a time, length and a Led By field that links any contact, whatever their type, or takes a typed name. Schedule items link Who's In Charge the same way. The contact's type shows beside each segment, and the Agenda prints from the Share page.
- **My Stuff.** The fourth button at the bottom (next to Home) gathers everything on your plate across every event: tasks assigned to you, grouped Overdue, Today, This Week and Coming Up, plus the agenda segments and schedule items you lead, the events you serve on, and the connection cards you follow up on. Switch to Created By Me to see the tasks you handed out. Filter to one event, or tap an event to jump in. It finds "you" by your login's email or name; if that misses, pick the contact that is you once. Copy or print your list, and from any contact's card, text or email them their own list.
- **Reminders.** My Stuff shows who needs a reminder: volunteers and meal sign-ups serving tomorrow, volunteers on an event that starts tomorrow, and anyone whose task is due today or tomorrow. Each one opens a text or email with the message already written, or email a whole shift at once by BCC. Sent reminders are marked so nobody gets two. With automatic sending set up (see `supabase/NOTIFY.md`) and **Automatic Reminders** on in Settings, they go out by themselves at 7 AM, and the list shows which ones already went.
- **Automatic texts and emails.** Once a sending service is connected (Resend for email, Twilio for texts; steps in `supabase/NOTIFY.md`), each login gets the notices they chose in Settings: new sign-ups and connection cards as they come in, tasks due, payments due, events a week and a day away, and a Monday summary. Settings shows whether sending is on and has a Send Me A Test button. Until it's connected, Settings says so and points to My Stuff.
- **Volunteer shifts.** Each volunteer spot is a shift with a role, a date and start and end times (6–7:30 PM), shown on the sign-up page and in reminders.
- **Background checks.** Each person in Contacts can have the date their background check cleared and, if it isn't the usual two years, when it expires. Mark a shift (kids, youth) as needing a check, and its card names anyone signed up who isn't cleared, isn't in Contacts yet (tap the name to add them), or whose check will have expired by the shift. The Overview lists them under Needs Attention, and the public sign-up page tells volunteers a check is required.
- **Back up your data.** Settings > Back Up Your Data downloads one .zip with a spreadsheet for each list (contacts, events, tasks, sign-ups, team, money, offerings, connection cards, prayer, schedule, agenda, equipment, budget) and a full copy of every record. Settings shows when this device last made one.
- **Self check-in.** The Share page has a Self Check-In link and QR code (`#/k/<event>`). Volunteers and guests scan it and enter the phone number they signed up with, and they are checked in for that day. The Overview's Check-In QR button puts it on screen at the welcome table.
- **Connection card follow-up.** Each card names who follows up (any contact, or a typed name) and by when. Cards show in that person's My Stuff. The Follow-Up list opens with decision counts (salvations, recommitments, prayer, church home, baptism, want to serve) and flags cards with no one assigned or past their date.
- **Event recap.** After an event, the Overview offers an Event Recap: attendance and check-ins, volunteers, decisions, money, tasks, answered prayer and notes for next year, on one page to share with the board or print.
- **Works without signal.** The app keeps a copy of your ministry's data on the device after every load. With no connection it opens from that copy, says when it was saved, and shows a banner; changes wait until you're back online.
- **Import contacts.** Directory > Import takes a spreadsheet saved as CSV (a header row with Name or First and Last Name, Phone, Email; Title, Organization, Address, Notes and Type are optional) or a .vcf export from a phone. It previews the list, skips people already in Contacts, and adds the rest as the type you choose or the file's Type column.
- **Weather.** For an event within two weeks that has an address, the Overview shows the forecast for each day (high, low and chance of rain) from Open-Meteo. Only the city and state are sent.
- **Desktop.** On a wide screen the bottom buttons become a sidebar and pages use more of the width.
- **Event day.** While an event is happening, its Overview opens with today's schedule and one-tap Check-In, Run Of Show and Phone List.
- **Notes for next year.** After an event ends, write down what worked and what to change. The notes stay with the event when you choose Start Next Year.
- **Add to calendar.** Public sign-up and RSVP pages have an Add To Calendar button.
- **Quick tour.** Settings > Help plays a narrated tour under two minutes long. Rebuild it with the scripts in `tour-src/` after the screens change.
- **Share and print.** The share button in an event's header opens one page with every link and QR code (volunteers, guest RSVP, connection card) and printable sheets: event summary, task list, run of show, volunteer roster, meal sign-ups, guest list, contact sheet, equipment checklist and payments owed. Everything you can share, links and QR codes included, can be copied as text, sent with the phone's share menu, or printed or saved as a PDF.
- **Ministry logo.** The owner uploads it in Settings. It is resized in the browser and shows in the app header, on share pages, on printed sheets and QR posters.
- **Annual events.** Open last year's event and choose **Start Next Year**: dates move to the new year, tasks and checklists reset, vendor payments reset, and sign-ups, volunteers and money are cleared (each is a checkbox). **Duplicate As A New Event** uses an event as a template for another.
- **Public pages** (no account): `#/v/<event>` for volunteer and meal sign-ups, `#/r/<event>` for guest RSVPs, `#/c/<event>` for a connection card, `#/k/<event>` for self check-in. Older `#/e/<event>` links still show everything.

## Files

- `index.html` holds the styles and loads the scripts.
- `data.js` is the data layer: `CloudDB` (Supabase) and `DemoDB` (browser storage, same API) plus the demo ministry.
- `app.js` is the screens.
- `supabase/functions/ed-notify/` sends the automatic texts and emails; `supabase/notify-schedule.sql` runs it every 15 minutes. See `supabase/NOTIFY.md`.
- `supabase/schema.sql` sets up the database. Paste it into Supabase's SQL Editor and run it; it is safe to run again. Every table starts with `ed_`, so it can share the Tend Home project without touching Tend's data. Row-level security keeps each ministry's rows to its own members, and the public pages go through a few `security definer` functions.
- `../home/supabase.js` is the Supabase client shared with Tend Home, and `qrcode.js` (MIT, Kazuhiko Arase) draws QR codes.
- Add `?demo` to the URL, or tap **Try The Demo** on the sign-in screen, to explore with sample data kept in the browser.
